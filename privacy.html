Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

OpenClaw Content Studio uses TikTok authorization only to provide user-approved content management and publishing functionality.

When a user connects a TikTok account, the service may receive basic account information and authorization tokens necessary to provide the requested TikTok integration.

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to operate the requested integration and is not sold to third parties.

Users control whether content is submitted to TikTok. Publication or other account actions require user authorization and approval.

Users may revoke TikTok authorization through their TikTok account settings.
